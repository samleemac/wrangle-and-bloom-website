Who's that girl?

Hi, I'm Sara, the crazy ideas wrangler behind Wrangle & Bloom.

I hold an MSc in Psychology and Neuroscience of Mental Health and a BSc (Hons) in Equine Science, and I've spent over 20 years with horses. I've not only travelled around the world but lived in many different cultures, and I bring calm to the chaos.

I'm a very hands-on person and don't mind getting them dirty in the process, whether that's setting up a business in the flesh or organising a team in an office, on (current) home turf or a remote island abroad. Everywhere is a possibility!
