Equine Freedomplicity

Hello

Equine Freedomplicity was born with the intention of helping you find the freedom and simplicity that we desire in our lives through the help of horses. Owner and founder, Sara de Jong, holds a MSc in Psychology and Neuroscience of Mental Health and a BSc (Hons) in Equine Science. Sara has also travelled and lived around the world throughout her life and is able to draw upon the different cultures and ways of life in different countries; paired with over 20 years of experience with horses. Mixing this all together with a healthy dose of desire to help people find their true selves and happiness is what will help you become the best version of yourself through improving your mental wellbeing.

Our Services

Equine Freedomplicity is now based in Galicia, Spain. Our programs are based on the latest scientific research and are tailored to help meet every individual’s goals. The sessions are non-ridden and are suitable to anyone, ranging from never having been around horses to our elite riders at the top of their sport. Ridden sessions can be organized upon request.

The types of sessions we can offer are listed below, but this is by no means an exhaustive list:

Elite Sports Coaching

Confidence Improvement

Anxiety Management

Corporate Team Building

Resilience Training

Programs

Though each program will be tailored to each individual and adaptations may need to be made on the day, we offer Regular and Advanced programs. If you’d rather focus on something else, get in touch so we can tailor a program for you and your needs.
