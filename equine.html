Hello

Equine Freedomplicity was born with the intention of helping you find the freedom and simplicity that we desire in our lives through the help of horses.

Owner and founder, Sara MacKinley-de Jong, holds a MSc in Psychology and Neuroscience of Mental Health and a BSc (Hons) in Equine Science. Sara has also travelled and lived around the world throughout her life and is able to draw upon the different cultures and ways of life in different countries; paired with over 20 years of experience with horses. Mixing this all together with a healthy dose of desire to help people find their true selves and happiness is what will help you become the best version of yourself through improving your mental wellbeing.

Our Services

Equine Freedomplicity is now based in Galicia, Spain. Sessions can be ridden or non-ridden and are tailored to suit anyone. Questions

I'm scared of horses. Can I still come?

Being scared of horses is a normal thing, after all they are quite big compared to us! This is something we will work on together. If you come to me, the horses are calm and well trained. Horses are natural prey animals, so they do not look to intentionally harm anyone, and they have even been classed as mirrors of people.

Do I need to ride, or have any experience with horses?

No. Sessions can be ridden or non-ridden and are tailored to suit anyone of any age and ability, from people who have never been around horses to elite riders at the top of their sport.

Where do sessions take place?

Equine Freedomplicity is based in Xermade, Galicia, Spain. I can come to you and we can use your horse, or if you don't have a horse, or would like to work with one of mine first, you can come to me.

Is there a fuel charge if you travel to me?

If I come to you, the same fuel charge applies to the taster and to every session. Up to 35km there is no extra cost. From 35km to 80km it is an additional €10. From 81km, we can discuss it.

What does a programme include?

Each programme is based around 10 sessions, using the latest scientific research, tailored to you (and the horse!) and adapted on the day where needed. You can pay per session, or pay for 10 upfront and get the 11th free.

How do I get started?

Using the link on this website you can book a half-price taster session. Half-price tasters are for new clients only. It's a chance to get to know me, talk about your goals, and see what Equine Freedomplicity has to offer and whether it is right for you. Send me a message and I'll reply within 2 working days.
