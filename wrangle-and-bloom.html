Projects that are still standing

Hanoi, Vietnam · 2014

Riding School in Vietnam

At 19 I was contracted to set up the first riding school in Hanoi, and to make sure it was financially sustainable and scalable. I had three months, no Vietnamese, and horses that had never been sat on, mares with foals at foot, and a few bought from the circus.

I trained the local staff in the how and the why of horse care until they could run it themselves, and helped set up a programme where horses help children with autism. The school is still going today.

Italy and the UK

Sports Horse Breeding ITA x UK

Eight months in Italy as project manager, setting up a sports horse breeding business that runs across Italy and the UK. That meant moving horses between countries, keeping every rule and regulation in order, and working with the different businesses that keep it all running.
